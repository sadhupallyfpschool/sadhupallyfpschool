● Code of conduct

School Rules

Simple, consistent expectations that keep our children safe, respectful and ready to learn — in the classroom and on the playground.

General conduct

  1. Every pupil must wear a clean, tidy uniform and arrive on time each day.

  2. Every pupil should greet their teacher politely at the beginning of the school day.

  3. When a teacher enters the classroom, pupils should stand quietly until asked to sit.

  4. No pupil may leave the classroom without the teacher's permission.

  5. Pupils should walk in an orderly, quiet line between assembly, classrooms and the playground.

  6. Writing on walls, damaging school property or disturbing classes is not allowed. Any damage should be reported to the teacher.

  7. All pupils are expected to keep their classroom neat and tidy.

  8. Pupils should listen attentively in class and complete their homework regularly.

  9. Kindness and courtesy towards classmates, teachers and staff are expected at all times.

🎒

Uniform & belongings

Children should wear the prescribed school uniform. Hair should be neat, nails trimmed, and books and belongings clearly labelled with their name and class.

⏰

Attendance & punctuality

Regular attendance matters, especially in the early years. Parents are requested to inform the school in case of leave or illness.

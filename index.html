From the Headmaster's Desk

"We measure our success in confident, curious children."

Sadhupally F.P. School was started with one simple aim — to give every child nearby a safe, caring place to take their first steps in education. We keep our classes small so every child is known by name, and we work closely with parents at every stage.

Welcome to Sadhupally F.P. School

We are a Pre-Primary to Class 4 school built around one idea: the early years shape everything that follows. Our teachers focus on strong basics in language and number, alongside the discipline, courtesy and curiosity children need to thrive later on.

● Good habits, early

A few rules every child learns

Simple, consistent expectations help children feel secure and learn respect for others.

  • Neat, clean uniform worn with care every day
  • Greet teachers and be punctual to school
  • Keep classrooms and school property neat and tidy
  • Walk, don't run, in corridors and stairways

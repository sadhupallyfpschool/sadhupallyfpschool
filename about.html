About Sadhupally F.P. School

Why we started, what we believe, and the people who guide our school every day.

Our history

Sadhupally F.P. School was founded to meet a simple, local need: a nearby school where young children could get a caring start to their education, from Pre-Primary through Class 4.

Since 1946, the school has grown class by class, keeping the same priorities — caring teachers, strong basic skills, reading, writing, numeracy and good habits.

Our mission

To give every child who walks through our gates a strong, confident foundation — academically, socially and personally — in a school that feels like an extension of home.

Headmaster's Desk

"Every child who leaves us after Class 4 should be a confident reader, a curious thinker, and a kind classmate."

We believe the first years of formal schooling help shape a child's future. Our teachers focus on discipline, courtesy, curiosity and learning.
